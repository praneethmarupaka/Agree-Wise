# AgreeWise Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

AgreeWise is a Chrome extension that helps users understand potential risks in Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policies of websites.

This Privacy Policy explains what information AgreeWise processes, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## 2. Information We Process

When you use AgreeWise, the extension may process:

- **Website policy URLs:** Links to Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policies detected on the webpage.
- **Policy text:** Text retrieved from those linked documents for analysis.
- **Website domain:** The domain associated with the website being analyzed.
- **Installation identifier:** A randomly generated identifier used for request limits and usage management.

AgreeWise does not require users to create an account.

## 3. How We Use Information

The information is used to:

- Retrieve and analyze website policies.
- Identify potentially concerning clauses.
- Generate a policy risk grade and explanations.
- Prevent duplicate analysis requests.
- Manage usage limits and improve service reliability.

## 4. AI Processing

AgreeWise sends extracted policy text to its backend server for analysis.

The backend uses Google's Gemini API to analyze the policy text and identify potentially concerning clauses.

The policy text is processed to provide the analysis results. Users should avoid submitting personal or confidential information intentionally.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

AgreeWise uses caching to reduce repeated processing:

- **Chrome extension:** Retrieved policy text and analysis results may be cached locally for up to 24 hours.
- **Backend:** Analysis results may be cached on the server for up to 30 days.

The extension also stores a randomly generated installation identifier for usage management.

## 6. Data Sharing

AgreeWise does not sell users' personal information.

Policy text submitted for analysis is shared with:

- AgreeWise's backend service provider, Render, for hosting and processing.
- Google Gemini API for AI-powered analysis.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 7. Permissions

AgreeWise requests Chrome permissions for:

- **Storage:** To save cached policy text, analysis results, and installation information.
- **Website access:** To detect relevant agreement links and retrieve linked policy documents on websites.

Website access is necessary for the extension's core functionality.

## 8. Security

AgreeWise uses reasonable technical measures to protect information during processing. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Children's Privacy

AgreeWise is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published with a revised date.
